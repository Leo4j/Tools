If this had been a real attack, you could have been redirected to a malicious website designed to:
  • Steal your Microsoft 365 credentials
  • Capture MFA codes
  • Collect payment card information
  • Install malware on your device
  • Compromise company systems

Why this matters

QR code phishing, also known as Quishing, is one of the fastest growing attack methods used by cyber criminals.

Unlike normal links, QR codes hide their destination and often bypass a user's normal security checks.

Remember

  • Verify the source before scanning
  • Check the destination URL carefully
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests
  • Treat QR codes exactly like hyperlinks
  • Never enter credentials unless you trust the destination
